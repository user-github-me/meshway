# Community code of conduct

Everyone may propose changes, report bugs and participate, regardless of
background, experience or identity. Be respectful, discuss the work, and accept
review feedback in good faith.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sharing credentials or private
traffic, and deliberately abusing peers or volunteer exits are unacceptable.
Do not test attacks against other people's devices or infrastructure without
their explicit authorization. Keep testing in owned, isolated environments.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban
participants when necessary. Responses should be proportionate; persistent or
serious abuse can result in immediate removal. Good-faith technical disagreement
and criticism of project decisions are welcome.

The maintainer is [@user-github-me](https://github.com/user-github-me). Report
public misconduct through GitHub's report feature or a sanitized issue addressed
to the maintainer. Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting for security
reports; never publish personal information as part of a complaint.
